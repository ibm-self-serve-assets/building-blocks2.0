# AI Engineering

AI Engineering provides the development layer that spans the entire software lifecycle — from planning and coding to testing, documentation, modernization, and CI/CD. Powered by **IBM Bob**, it gives developers an AI-native partner that accelerates every phase of software delivery across all building blocks on this platform.

Two forces are reshaping how enterprises build and maintain software. The first is the rise of agentic AI — where autonomous agents, orchestration systems, and AI-driven workflows are now core engineering deliverables, not experiments. The second is the weight of legacy — decades of business logic embedded in Java monoliths, mainframe COBOL, IBM Z, and IBM i systems that enterprises cannot abandon but cannot afford to leave untouched.

**Agentic SDLC** addresses the first: giving development teams an IDE-native AI partner that handles every phase of building new software — from requirements and architecture through code generation, testing, documentation, and CI/CD — so teams ship faster without sacrificing quality or governance.

**Code Modernization** addresses the second: using IBM Bob's structured transformation workflows to analyze legacy codebases, map dependencies, apply incremental migrations, and validate that business logic survives the transition — turning multi-year modernization programmes into repeatable, AI-accelerated delivery.

Together, they cover the full engineering surface — building what's next while systematically retiring what's holding the enterprise back.
